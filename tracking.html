How We Track

Every box of food takes the same four steps, from a school’s share table to a community fridge. Here’s each one, and how it ends up on the public record.

Follow a box ↓

  1. Step 1

    Collect sealed leftovers

    At the end of lunch, student volunteers gather the unopened, packaged food left on their school’s share table.

    • Sealed and unopened only
    • At our partner schools

  2. Step 2

    Pack it in a cold box

    The food is weighed and packed into an insulated box with an ice pack, which keeps it at or colder. The box gets its own tracking ID, written on its label.

    • Ice pack in every box
    • Weighed before it leaves
    • ID like STB-7K3Q9M

  3. Step 3

    Walk it to a community fridge

    Students walk the box to a community fridge near their school the same afternoon, and the drop-off is confirmed on the spot.

    • A fridge near the school
    • Same-afternoon handoff

  4. Step 4

    Post it online

    The volunteer logs the delivery in our member portal: the date, the weight, the foods, and where it came from and went. An admin checks it, and then it counts on the public delivery log.

    • Checked by an admin
    • Public for anyone to see

    The public log shows each delivery’s date, weight, number of foods and the school it came from. Volunteers’ names stay private.
