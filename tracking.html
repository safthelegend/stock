The fine print

How the temperature loggers work.

Every box should carry a temperature record from packing to handoff, so a food bank can trust what we bring and a school can show exactly what left its building. NYC health rules require that record.11

We plan to share about three temperature loggers across all routes, instead of one in every box. A box that travels without a logger has no temperature record, and the log will say so. We had planned to estimate those trips from past data, but that is not possible until real trips have been logged. The boxes do not track location.

Want to build a logger yourself? The parts list and diagram are on the Getting started page.
