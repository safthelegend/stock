About this map

The shading and the school pins are real. The spread you see when you press play is a “what if”: it shows what a student-run network could look like across the city, not anything that has happened. None of the pale dots is a real school.

Shading shows the percent of residents getting SNAP in each community district, from NYC Council Data Team analysis of NYC Human Resources Administration and NYC Department of City Planning data, September 2025. It is not a map of where we operate. One food bank partner site is being set up, in Manhattan.

School pins are placed from public campus addresses and have not been checked against a geocoder; run tools/geocode.mjs to regenerate them from a live service. The receiving site’s exact location is not published, so its pin is borough-level.

This site runs no analytics, sets no cookies and carries no trackers. It stores up to two small notes in this browser's localStorage, and neither ever leaves your device: your colour-theme choice, and, only if you're a Stock the Block member who has signed in here, a note that turns the Sign in button into My portal. Signing out removes it. The member sign-in itself runs on Google Firebase and loads only on the member portal, never on the public pages; while you're signed in there, Firebase keeps your sign-in in this browser until you sign out. Two outside services are involved on this page, and they're worth naming. The map loads its background tiles from CARTO and OpenStreetMap, so opening it sends your IP address and the map area you're viewing to those services. The contact form is run by Google Firebase: this page loads Firebase's code from Google's servers, and anything you send through the form is stored in our Firebase database, where only our team can read it. Everything else here is served from this site.
