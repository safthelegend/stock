Why we exist

Built by the students who watched the share table get thrown out.

We started this because the food on our share tables was sealed, safe, and a short walk from people who needed it. Students built the boxes, the temperature logger and the tracker, and students will walk the routes and keep the logs.

If your school has a share table and a food bank within walking distance, it can run there too.
