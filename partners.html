Partners & Press

Stock the Block only works because other people say yes: the schools that let us collect from their share tables, the fridges and pantries that take the food, and the organizations that back us. This is everyone we work with.

Partner organizations

Nonprofits that back our work.

Partner

Grassroots Grocery

A New York nonprofit fighting for dignified food access for all. Every Saturday, its volunteers rescue unsold fresh produce from the Hunts Point Market and bring it to neighbors who need it, through community-led food sites and community fridges: more than 3,000,000 pounds of produce so far.

Visit grassrootsgrocery.org →

Partner

CAMBA

A Brooklyn nonprofit that began in 1977 as a small merchant association in Flatbush. Today its 170+ programs help more than 70,000 New Yorkers a year with housing, health, education, legal aid, and food: its Beyond Hunger Emergency Food Pantry serves nearly 16,000 people a month.

Visit camba.org →

Partner

ICNA Relief

A national nonprofit based in New Hyde Park, New York. Its Hunger Prevention program runs more than 46 food pantries across the country, serving thousands of people every month, along with hot-meal deliveries for neighbors who can’t reach them and food for children after school and on weekends.

Visit icnarelief.org →

Partner

One Love Community Fridge

A New York nonprofit bringing fresh, healthy food to its neighbors, led by “Respect, Dignity, and Health.” Since 2021 it has redirected more than 12 million pounds of excess food and served over 1 million meals. Its fridges in Flatbush and Gowanus are among the ones we stock.

Visit onelovecommunityfridge.org →

Partner

Cafeteria Culture

A New York nonprofit that, since 2009, has turned school cafeterias into hubs for student action on food waste and plastic pollution. Its Mindful Choice Meals cut cafeteria food waste by 35 to 50 percent, and its Plastic Free Lunch Day now reaches more than 4,000 schools across the country.

Visit cafeteriaculture.org →

Endorsement

Larry He for State Senate

Endorsed by Larry He for New York State Senate, backing our students’ work getting sealed, unopened surplus school food to the New Yorkers who need it.

Press

The Washington Post

Our founder’s letter in The Washington Post answered the opinion piece “How SNAP soda reform is improving health” with what we see on the ground: SNAP cuts have devastated families, and within hours of a food drop-off, the fridges at every one of our receiving sites are empty.
