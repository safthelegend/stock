Team Roles

What each role on the team is responsible for.

  • Founder

    Sets program direction, leads partnerships with schools and cafeteria staff, and oversees all teams.

  • Mentor & CUNY Expansion Lead

    Advises the student team and oversees operations across sites, and leads expansion of the program to CUNY campuses.

  • Logistics Lead

    Plans pickup schedules and routes, and coordinates student volunteers between partner schools and pantries.

  • Finance Lead

    Builds and tracks the program budget, including hardware, coolers, and supplies, and leads grant applications and the search for a fiscal sponsor.

  • Outreach Lead

    Manages relationships with partner pantries and new schools, handles first contact and follow-ups, and leads expansion to new neighborhoods.

  • Engineering Co-Lead

    Builds and maintains the cold-chain temperature loggers and the Track the Box logging platform.

  • Health & Safety Lead

    Maintains food handling standards and makes sure pickups follow cold-chain and safety protocol.

  • Communications Lead

    Runs internal team updates, social media, and volunteer recruiting materials.

  • Transport Lead

    Handles physical pickups and deliveries, prepares coolers and gel packs, and makes sure manifests are signed at drop-off. Reports to the Logistics Lead.

  • Brooklyn Site Operator

    Runs day-to-day operations at the Brooklyn partner school, coordinating collection and handoff to the partner pantry, and serves as the site's point of contact.

  • Collection Coordinator

    Collects sealed surplus from school share tables, sorts and packs items for transport, and logs each box in Track the Box.
